## Meteo

- **Air pressure** (PA): PAA-33X, Keller AG, Switzerland
- **Air temperature** (TA): HygroClip, Rotronic AG, Switzerland
- **Photosynthetic photon flux density** (PPFD): PAR sensor, Kipp & Zonen, The Netherlands
- **Precipitation** (PREC): 2005–2016: rain gage, Toss GmbH, Potsdam, Germany; March 2016–2020: rain gage, Lambrecht meteo GmbH, Germany
- **Radiation balance** (SW_IN, SW_OUT, LW_IN, LW_OUT): CNR1, Kipp & Zonen, The Netherlands
- **Relative humidity** (RH): HygroClip, Rotronic AG, Switzerland
- **Soil temperature** (TS): TB107, Markasub AG, Switzerland
- **Soil water content** (SWC): ML2x, Delta-T Devices Ltd, United Kingdom

## Data loggers

Data were recorded by data loggers taking one measurement every 10s: 
- 2005 to 2016: CR10X-2 M; 2016 to 2020: CR1000
- since March 2020: CR1000X, Campbell Scientific, UT, USA

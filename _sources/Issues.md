# Known Issues

## Fluxes

### Few directly measured fluxes for N<sub>2</sub>O and CH<sub>4</sub> in 2017, 2018 and 2022
- For N<sub>2</sub>O and CH<sub>4</sub>, only few directly measured fluxes were available in 2017, 2018 and in the second half of 2022. The gaps during these years were still gap-filled, however, the gap-filling models likely overestimated the emissions during these time periods. Therefore, these gap-filled fluxes must be taken with a grain of salt, especially when calculating complete yearly budgets for these years.
